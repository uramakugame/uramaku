# Loot, Income and owner-control corrections

- Loot's explicit off-turn exception has been removed from the engine and the browser. During Chaos it replaces Earn as your normal action, transfers exactly one coin and consumes that turn. Off-turn requests, repeat clicks after the turn passes, spectators and every pending claim/counter/information/loss/Summon decision are rejected without changing coins, room revision or SQLite state. The English/Japanese rules now match.
- Income keeps its 1475 ms coin badge, but the badge is a cancelable nonblocking decoration. The new player's actions are available immediately after the prior action and 520 ms handoff finish. Later action/challenge snapshots do not wait for the Income badge. Action costs/transfers, attacks, card reveals/replacements and handoff sequencing remain blocking. Room changes still remove Income effects.
- The active-match owner control is named **Kick player**. It enables one-target selection, supports cancellation and requires confirmation. Connected and disconnected opponents still holding influences are eligible. The server uses the normal forfeit/continuation and coin-refund rules, revokes the reconnect token, marks the seat offline and clears a live socket binding. Authorization, transaction rollback and outstanding challenge penalties remain enforced.
- The lobby has its previous direct per-player Kick buttons and no selection-mode toggle. The existing compact offline text and Kick button colors/padding return, right-aligned beside Coins using overlays that cannot add a card row. Eliminated players lose the kick affordance.
- Match information and the deck/discard area are borderless. Match information uses typography, spacing and a subtle faded rule with the existing Order/Chaos accents. Individual card edges, approved portraits/card backs, the new Loot/Overthrow icons and Latest outcome remain unchanged.

Automated checks cover real game/socket/SQLite guards, the real EN/JP multiplayer renderer and action controls during an unfinished Income animation, connected/offline kick authorization/revocation/rollback and existing card/turn sequencing.
